Galaxy surveys are how we turn the sky into a catalogue. Each one trades off area against depth
against wavelength coverage, and each generation has set the stepping stone for the next. This is a
rough map of when the major ones ran, or are planned to run.

I have split them three ways: surveys that measure spectra and so get precise redshifts, surveys
that image the sky in broad bands, and the ones that do both.

## Sky coverage and depth

The other axis that matters is how much sky a survey covers and how faint it goes. Those two pull
against each other for any fixed amount of telescope time, which is why the surveys above occupy
such different niches rather than simply superseding one another.
