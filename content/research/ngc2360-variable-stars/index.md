This project presents a search for new variable stars in the open cluster NGC 2360 using
photometry. MaxIm DL was used to calibrate the images. The raw images were taken by
Dr David Mkrtichian, now a science member at NARIT, on the 0.5 m telescope at the Thai
National Observatory (TNO) in Chiang Mai, on 24, 25 and 27 December 2014.

The calibrated images were analysed to search for variable stars not yet identified in
international databases such as SIMBAD and VizieR. We used SigmaPlot to determine the periods
of the variable stars from a time-series wave function, and took colour index data from SIMBAD
to estimate their spectral types.

We found four stars that appear to be variable. Stars 1&ndash;3 have periods of 3.24, 4.50 and
3.84 hours respectively, and all three are roughly spectral type F. The fourth has a period of
1.69 hours and is not yet identified in any database — so it may be the newest variable star
detected using TNO.
